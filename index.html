About the conference

Where applied mathematics meets computing

Applied Sciences and Computing Engineering is a broad, multidisciplinary field spanning engineering, numerical methods, applied mathematics, and computer science. RTASCE exists to bring senior and early-career researchers together to exchange ideas and confront the field's open problems, with an eye toward engineering solutions with genuine global reach.

Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished research across the themes listed below. The programme runs across three days and includes keynote addresses, paper-presentation tracks, panel discussions, and talks from international and national experts — built for lasting collaboration between fellow researchers.
